Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation Phase
Project Title: EduGenie AI – Your Smart Learning Companion
Project Overview: EduGenie AI is an artificial intelligence-powered educational platform designed to act as a virtual tutor and study planner. It helps students optimize their learning by generating personalized study schedules, simplifying complex academic concepts, and recommending targeted learning resources.

Problem Statement:

Students frequently struggle with time management and creating structured study routines.

Access to immediate, personalized tutoring for complex subjects is often expensive or unavailable.

Learners experience information overload and have difficulty finding concise, verified academic resources.

Proposed Solution:

AI Study Planner: Automatically generates tailored timetables based on exam dates, syllabus size, and user availability.

Smart Concept Simplifier: Integrates with generative AI to break down complex technical topics into easy-to-understand summaries.

Resource Curator: Recommends verified articles, coding documentation, and video links based on the user's current learning topic.

Target Audience: High school students, university undergraduates, and self-taught professionals.
